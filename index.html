Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF) · AUDP-DSP

Software-defined signal processing for agricultural research.

AUDP-DSP generates, analyzes, transforms, and reconstructs numerical signals and waveforms for research into agricultural sensing, measurement data, and related scientific applications. It is developed by Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF) as part of its agriculture research and innovation work.

What it is

Agricultural instruments and sensors produce measurement signals: soil moisture time series, water levels, weather readings, machinery vibration, and data from aerial platforms. AUDP-DSP is the software layer that supports research into representing, analyzing, filtering, and reconstructing these signals using mathematical methods.

Generate

Create mathematically defined signals and waveforms for controlled research experiments.

Analyze

Study frequency content, amplitude, phase, energy, and statistical properties.

Transform

Apply filtering, Fourier methods, and reconstruction techniques for research.

Research approach

The platform emphasizes mathematical clarity, reproducible numerical behavior, and clear separation between measured observations, numerical signal representations, and derived analytical results. Detailed algorithms, source structure, and internal specifications are not published on this site. They remain in private repositories under controlled access.
