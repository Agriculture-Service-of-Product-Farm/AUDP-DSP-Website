Software-Defined Signal Processing

AUDP-DSP is a research and engineering platform developed by Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF) for mathematical signal processing in support of agricultural sensing, waveform analysis, and related scientific applications.

About ASPF

Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF) is a research-oriented organization focused on agriculture solutions, including consultancy, precision hardware, compliance support, software services, and data-driven approaches for farming and related scientific work. Headquarters are in Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh, India. Official site: apservicefarm.com.

AUDP-DSP Focus Areas

Signal Generation

Creation of mathematically defined signals and waveforms for research and simulation.

Signal Analysis

Study of frequency, amplitude, phase, energy, and statistical properties of signals.

Transformation

Filtering, Fourier-based methods, convolution, and multirate processing for research use.

Agricultural Sensing

Support for processing sensor and measurement data relevant to agricultural applications.

Simulation

Controlled numerical experiments involving noise, sampling, and channel effects.

Research Platform

Independent software-defined framework that can support later integration with broader ASPF systems.
