How it works

AUDP-DSP supports research into representing measurement data as numerical signals, applying mathematical transformations, and examining the results. The descriptions below are high-level; detailed algorithms and implementations are not published here.

Signal representation

Measurements from sensors are represented as numerical samples with explicit sampling information and metadata. This allows research into time-series analysis, frequency content, and reconstruction.

Generation

Mathematically defined waveforms can be generated for controlled experiments. These support testing of analysis methods under known conditions.

Analysis and transformation

Research methods include statistical summaries, frequency-domain analysis, filtering, and multiresolution techniques. Results are intended to be traceable to defined models and validated procedures.

Reconstruction

Where applicable, signals can be reconstructed from transformed representations so that numerical error can be measured and controlled.

No source code, numerical contracts, or internal specifications are disclosed on this page.
