Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF)

Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF) is a research-oriented organization focused on agriculture solutions. Its work includes consultancy for farming practices, precision hardware, compliance support, software and services, and data-driven approaches for farming and related scientific work.

ASPF is headquartered in Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh, India. Official site: apservicefarm.com.

Research programmes

ASPF develops and maintains several research platforms. AUDP-DSP is the software-defined signal processing platform focused on mathematical analysis of measurement and waveform data. Related public information is available for other ASPF work where published.

  • Precision farming technology and sensor data handling
  • Sustainable agriculture practices
  • Software and data-driven decision support
  • Mathematical foundations for signal analysis and information processing

AUDP-DSP

AUDP-DSP is a software-defined signal processing research platform developed under ASPF. It supports research into mathematical signal processing for agricultural sensing and related measurement applications. The core implementation remains private.
